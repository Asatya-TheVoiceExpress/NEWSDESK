Blotter

there is no information in truth

Every third day, one real reported year from the National Crime Records Bureau's record of crimes against women in India is plotted as algorithmic art and set beside a haiku. The NCRB reports annually and lags, so there is no monthly figure to plot and the column does not invent one: it reads backwards through the record instead, and every entry states which year it is reading. The plate is a rendering of a number, not an illustration of anything: nothing in it is a picture of an event, and no image here depicts a person or a case. What it draws is the number's surprisal — how much information the figure carries against the other years in the record — so a year that falls exactly where the rest predict draws almost nothing at all. On these four years it draws very little. That is the column's argument, and its title: there is no information in truth.
